# What's new

## 1.2.5

**Calendar**

- **Delete an event from its page.** Tap an event to open it: there's now a red **Delete event** button at the bottom. It asks before deleting (a repeating event is deleted with all its repeats). Swiping an event to the left still works too.
- **See the next 30 days at once.** Tap the selected day in the week strip again to unselect it. The list then shows every event from now until 30 days ahead, under a heading for each day. Tap a day's heading to go to that day, or **TODAY** to go back.
- **Agenda widget looks further ahead.** It now shows your next events over the coming 30 days instead of only this week, so it's no longer empty after a quiet week. It still has room for five events, and says "Nothing in the next 30 days" when there are none.

The Help guide describes the new calendar features.
