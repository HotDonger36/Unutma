# What's new

## 1.3.0

**Calendar widget**

- **The Agenda widget scrolls.** Swipe up and down to see every event in the next 30 days, not just the first five. Tapping an event opens the calendar.
- **The month is shown.** Day headings now read "WED 18 NOV", and the Next event widget says "Wed 18 Nov" too.

**Language**

- **Português (Portugal).** Settings › Appearance › Language switches the app between English and Portuguese (PT-PT). Dates, months and weekdays follow it. The Help guide and your own notes stay as written; anything not translated yet shows in English.

**Notes**

- **Hold to select, handle to move.** Tapping a note opens it, holding it selects it, and moving is now separate: once you are selecting, every note shows a round tick box on the left and a drag handle on the right. Drag the handle to reorder.

**Pinned to the notification bar**

- **A finished checklist leaves the bar.** When every item of a pinned checklist is ticked, its notification goes away by itself. When the checklist resets (daily, weekly or custom routines) or you untick something, it comes back.

**Reminders**

- **Pick your own reminder sound** from the sounds on your phone (Settings › Notifications › Reminder sound), and optionally **play it as an alarm**, so it is loud even when the phone is on silent.

## 1.2.5

**Calendar**

- **Delete an event from its page.** Tap an event to open it: there's now a red **Delete event** button at the bottom. It asks before deleting (a repeating event is deleted with all its repeats). Swiping an event to the left still works too.
- **See the next 30 days at once.** Tap the selected day in the week strip again to unselect it. The list then shows every event from now until 30 days ahead, under a heading for each day. Tap a day's heading to go to that day, or **TODAY** to go back.
- **Agenda widget looks further ahead.** It now shows your next events over the coming 30 days instead of only this week, so it's no longer empty after a quiet week. It still has room for five events, and says "Nothing in the next 30 days" when there are none.

The Help guide describes the new calendar features.
